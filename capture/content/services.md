### COMPREHENSIVE CONNECTIVITY SOLUTIONS

Peace Country Installations provides reliable Starlink, cellular booster, and remote communication solutions across Northern Alberta. We specialize in professional installations, affordable equipment rentals for temporary projects, flexible lease to own programs, and custom connectivity solutions for homes, businesses, job sites, farms, and off-grid locations.

#### Built for Northern Alberta

We pride ourselves on delivering competitive pricing, quality workmanship, and dependable local support that helps keep Northern Alberta connected.

Whether you're working in the field, living off-grid, running a business, or simply need better connectivity, Peace Country Installations offers reliable solutions tailored to your needs and budget. We specialize in Lease to Own Financing to get you connected affordably, and Equipment Rentals for temporary projects, work camps, events, and remote operations.

##### Starlink Supply & Installation

Professional mounting, routing, and setup of Starlink hardware for optimal speeds and reliability.

##### Starlink Equipment Rentals

Short-term and long-term rental options for job sites, events, and temporary remote setups.

##### Starlink Lease to Own Programs

Affordable monthly payment plans to get you connected without the massive upfront hardware costs.

##### Cellular Signal Booster Installation

Boost weak cell signals in your home, shop, or office to ensure clear calls and reliable data.

##### Remote Communication Solutions

Custom-engineered connectivity for the most isolated locations in Northern Alberta.

##### Residential Connectivity Solutions

Robust home networks, Wi-Fi extensions, and reliable rural internet for your family.

##### Commercial Connectivity Solutions

Enterprise-grade networks and failover systems to keep your business running smoothly.

##### Farm & Agricultural Connectivity

Connect outbuildings, shops, and smart farming equipment across your entire property.

##### Job Site Internet Solutions

Rugged, deployable internet solutions for construction camps, oilfield sites, and remote crews.

##### Off-Grid Communication Systems

Solar-powered and low-draw communication setups for true off-grid living and operations.

#### Ready to Get Connected?

Serving Grande Prairie and surrounding communities throughout Northern Alberta. Contact us today to discuss your specific connectivity needs.
